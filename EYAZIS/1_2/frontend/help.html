Help & Documentation

Navigation Guide

Search

Main page with the search bar. Enter your query to search through the document corpus using cosine similarity. Results are ranked by relevance score.

Upload

Add text documents to the corpus. You can enter text manually or upload .txt files. Documents are automatically indexed for searching.

Documents

Browse all indexed documents in the corpus. Click on any document to view its full content. Delete documents you no longer need.

Metrics

Evaluate search quality using standard IR metrics. Enter queries with known relevant documents to calculate Precision, Recall, and F-Score.
