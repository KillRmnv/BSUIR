A music instrument is a device created or adapted to make musical sounds. Musical instruments can be classified into several categories including string, wind, percussion, and electronic instruments. Common string instruments include the guitar, violin, and piano. Wind instruments include the flute, saxophone, and trumpet. Percussion instruments include drums, cymbals, and xylophones. Each instrument has unique tonal qualities and requires specific techniques to play. Learning to play a musical instrument improves cognitive abilities, coordination, and emotional expression.
