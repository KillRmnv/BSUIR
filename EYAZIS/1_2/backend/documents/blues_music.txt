Blues music originated in the Deep South of the United States during the late 19th century. It evolved from African American work songs, spirituals, and field hollers. Blues is characterized by specific chord progressions, blue notes, and a call and response pattern. The genre has had a profound influence on the development of jazz, rock, and pop music. Iconic blues musicians include Robert Johnson, B.B. King, and Muddy Waters. Blues music continues to be performed and celebrated at festivals and venues around the world.
