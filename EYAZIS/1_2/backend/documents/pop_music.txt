Pop music is a genre of popular music that originated in its modern form during the mid-1950s in the United States and the United Kingdom. Pop music is characterized by a catchy melody, verse-chorus structure, and repetitive hooks. The genre draws from various styles including rock, urban, dance, and Latin music. Artists like Michael Jackson, Madonna, and Beyonce have defined pop music across different decades. Pop music dominates commercial radio and streaming platforms, making it the most widely consumed music genre globally.
