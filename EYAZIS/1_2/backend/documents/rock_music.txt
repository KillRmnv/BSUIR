Rock music emerged in the United States during the late 1940s and early 1950s. It originated from genres like gospel, country, and rhythm and blues. Rock music is characterized by a strong beat, electric guitars, and a bass guitar. The genre has evolved into many subgenres including classic rock, punk rock, and alternative rock. Artists like Elvis Presley, The Beatles, and Led Zeppelin are considered pioneers of rock music. Rock concerts attract millions of fans worldwide and remain one of the most popular forms of live entertainment.
