Jazz music originated in African American communities in New Orleans during the late 19th and early 20th centuries. It is characterized by swing and blue notes, complex chords, call and response vocals, and polyrhythms. Jazz has roots in blues and ragtime music. Key instruments in jazz include the saxophone, trumpet, piano, and double bass. Notable jazz musicians include Louis Armstrong, Duke Ellington, Miles Davis, and John Coltrane. Jazz has influenced many other music genres and is often considered America's classical music due to its cultural significance and artistic depth.
