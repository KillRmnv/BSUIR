Electronic music is a genre that uses electronic instruments and technology for its production. It emerged in the mid-20th century with the development of synthesizers and computer technology. Electronic music encompasses many styles including house, techno, trance, and dubstep. The genre relies heavily on digital audio workstations, drum machines, and software synthesizers. Pioneers of electronic music include Kraftwerk, Brian Eno, and Aphex Twin. Today, electronic dance music festivals attract hundreds of thousands of attendees and generate billions in revenue worldwide.
