The music industry encompasses the businesses and organizations involved in the production, distribution, and sale of music. It includes record labels, music publishers, concert promoters, and streaming services. The industry has undergone significant transformation with the rise of digital distribution and streaming platforms. Major record labels like Universal Music Group, Sony Music Entertainment, and Warner Music Group dominate the commercial music market. Independent artists now have more opportunities to distribute their music through platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and Bandcamp.
