Classical music is a broad term that refers to Western art music traditions. It encompasses a wide range of periods and styles including Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and Modern eras. Composers such as Johann Sebastian Bach, Ludwig van Beethoven, and Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart are central figures in classical music. Classical music is typically performed by orchestras, chamber groups, or solo instrumentalists. The genre emphasizes complex harmonic structures, dynamic range, and formal compositional techniques. Many film scores and contemporary compositions draw heavily from classical music traditions.
