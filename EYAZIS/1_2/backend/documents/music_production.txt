Music production is the process of creating a music recording from start to finish. It involves several stages including pre-production, recording, mixing, and mastering. Modern music production relies heavily on digital audio workstations such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro, and FL Studio. Producers work with artists to shape the sound and arrangement of songs. The quality of music production significantly impacts the final sound and commercial success of a recording. Many producers have become famous in their own right, such as Quincy Jones, Dr. Dre, and Rick Rubin.
