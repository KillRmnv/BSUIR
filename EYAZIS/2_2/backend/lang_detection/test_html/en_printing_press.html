Printing Press

The printing press with movable type was developed by Johannes Gutenberg around 1440. It allowed books to be produced hundreds of times faster than by hand copying. Cheap printed texts spread literacy and new ideas across Europe within decades. Historians consider the press one of the most influential inventions in history.
