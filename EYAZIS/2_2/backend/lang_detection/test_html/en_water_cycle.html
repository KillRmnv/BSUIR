Water Cycle

The water cycle describes how water moves between oceans, atmosphere, and land. Heat from the Sun evaporates surface water, and rising vapour cools to form clouds. Precipitation returns water to rivers and lakes, from where it flows back to the sea. Underground reservoirs, called aquifers, store fresh water for wells and springs.
