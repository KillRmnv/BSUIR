Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower was built for the World Exhibition held in Paris in 1889. Its iron lattice reaches a height of three hundred and thirty metres, antenna included. Initially criticised by artists, it gradually became the most recognisable symbol of France. Millions of visitors climb its stairs or take lifts to the observation decks every year.
